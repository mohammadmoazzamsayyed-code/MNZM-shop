Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ❓

1. What is the fabric quality and material used? ➕
All our plazos, leggings, and bottomwear are crafted from 100% pure premium cotton and breathable 4-way stretch fabric. Designed for all-day maximum comfort and long-lasting durability.
2. How long does delivery take? ➕
We offer **Same-Day / 24-Hour Dispatch** for local orders across Sakinaka and surrounding Mumbai areas. Orders to other regions in Mumbai are delivered within 1–2 business days.
3. Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available? ➕
Yes! We support both Cash on Delivery (COD) and Online UPI Payments (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) for a seamless shopping experience.
4. What is your Order Cancellation policy? ➕
You can easily cancel or modify your order within **2 hours** of placing it by clicking the "Cancel Order 🚫" link on our website or contacting us directly on WhatsApp.
